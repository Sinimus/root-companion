# Rules fix against the Law of Root (Oct 2025), second round

Follow-up to `audit-2026-09-27-law.md` and `fix-2026-10-02-law.md`. Branch `server/root-dev/sprint-3-fixes`, 2026-10-16 and 17.

## Sources

- **Law**: `src/data/law_full.ts` (generated from `Root_Base_Law_Oct_2025.pdf`, October 13, 2025).
- **Cards and FAQ**: the Leder card library and the official rulebooks as indexed for retrieval (card texts by id, for example ROOT-51 Armorers, ROOT-77 Fox Partisans).
- Everything else (board photographs, card images, the Law PDF itself) was not available, so claims that need them stay open.

## Audit status

Every item of the 2026-09-27 audit was checked against the current code. 172 of 176 rows (175 audit items plus one "no errors" note) were already fixed; one was not (the Recruit glossary reference, fixed now); three cannot be decided from the Law text: the Winter, Autumn and Lake board layouts, and the crafting costs of five cards still marked `['?']`.

## Open items

- **Maps**: the Winter, Autumn and Lake layouts and slot counts still rest on photographs; the Law gives only totals.
- **Copies per card** (`totalInDeck`): the Law gives copy counts only for ambush (2.1.2) and dominance (2.1.3) cards, which are set. Other counts need the physical deck list.
- **Crafting costs** of Royal Claim, Boat Builders, Corvid Planners, Propaganda Bureau and Saboteurs are `['?']`; they are on the card images only.
- **Draw bonus tracks**: which faction-board track carries the draw bonuses is on the board images, not in the Law.
- **Reach calculator and hirelings**: the calculator does not exclude a faction whose hireling is in play (A.6.5); the draft does.
- **AdSet draft**: no option for the second Vagabond setup card (A.8.1), and the Vagabond character cards are not dealt (A.8.2.III).
- **Armorers** is offered to the defender only; the card ("In battle, may discard this to ignore all rolled hits taken") works for either side.
- **Homeland factions** (chapters 16 to 18) and the Knaves, Diaspora and Twilight Council setup cards are not in the app.
