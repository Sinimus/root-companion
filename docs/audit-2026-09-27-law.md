## Sources and conventions

- **Law** = `Root_Base_Law_Oct_2025.md` (The Law of Root, October 13, 2025). Page = nearest preceding `<!-- p.N -->` marker.
- **Cards** = `leder_cards.md` (official Leder card list, `ROOT-id | name | product | tags | text`).
- **LTP** = the Learn to Play booklets (secondary, used only where the Law is silent).
- The Law of Rootbotics was not needed: the app contains no bot content.
- Extraction quirk: the headings "6.4 Birdsong" and "12.4 Birdsong" were lost. Their text sits under the 6.5 heading (p.6) and the 12.5 heading (p.14). Citations below name the real section.

### What the sources cannot verify

The extracted sources do not contain: hireling card texts and landmark card texts (their rows in Cards are empty), crafting costs and copy counts of shared-deck cards, item VP values, the Reach table (5.2 keeps only the prose), clearing-by-clearing map layouts, and which faction-board track carries the draw bonuses (the Law says "uncovered draw bonus"). Claims that rest only on these are listed as **uncertain**, never as errors.

### Severity labels

- **wrong rule**: the app states a mechanic the Law contradicts.
- **wrong number**: right mechanic, wrong quantity.
- **wrong reference**: `ruleReference` or glossary reference points at the wrong Law section.
- **outdated edition**: matches an older Law edition (the one copied into law_full.ts), not Oct 2025.

Counting: one numbered item per wrong step or field. A claim repeated in several files is listed in each file and marked "same as ...".

## guides/vagabond.ts

1. **wrong rule** (setup step 2). App: "Take starting items: 1 Boot, 1 Sword, 1 Torch, 1 Hammer, 1 Coin, 1 Bag." Law 9.3.5 (p.10): "Take the items marked with an 'S' listed on your character card." Appendix V (p.28-29) gives different sets per character, e.g. V.1.1 Thief "Start with M, F, T, S" (boot, torch, tea, sword), V.2.1 Tinker "M, F, B, H" (boot, torch, bag, hammer), V.3.1 Ranger "M, F, C, S" (boot, torch, crossbow, sword), V.4.1 Vagrant "X, F, M", V.5.1 Arbiter "M, F, S, S", V.6.1 Scoundrel "M, M, F, C". No character starts with the app's six. Fix: add a "choose character" step (9.3.1) and take items from the chosen character.
2. **wrong rule** (setup step 3). App: "Place all starting items in your satchel (face up/exhausted)." Law 9.3.5 (p.10): "Place any listed T, X, and B items face up on the respective tracks of your faction board. Place all other listed items face up in your Satchel." Items start face up (ready), not exhausted, and tea, coins and bags go on tracks. Fix accordingly.
3. **wrong rule** (setup step 5). App: "Place your quest token on the quest track." Law 9.3.3 (p.10): "Shuffle your quest deck, draw 3 quest cards, and place them face up near you." There is no quest token or track. Fix: replace the step; also add 9.3.4 (populate the ruins with the four "R" items).
4. **wrong rule** (Refresh). App: "Flip 3 exhausted items face up. Flip +2 more for each Tea in satchel." Law 9.4.1 (p.10): "Flip two exhausted items face up for each T face up on the Refresh track, not counting T that you flip face up in this step. Then flip up three more exhausted items." Fix: count face-up tea on the Refresh track, not in the Satchel.
5. **wrong rule** (Slip, description and tip). App: "Ignore all restrictions." Law 9.4.2 (p.10): the Slip is free of boots "even for moving into a Hostile clearing" and "ignores all effects that prevent movement out of a clearing". It does not ignore effects that prevent moving into a clearing (for example V.6.2 Scorched Earth: "Pieces cannot be placed in or moved into the clearing", p.28). Fix: "Free move, no boot even into Hostile clearings; ignores effects that block leaving a clearing."
6. **wrong number** (Move). App: "Exhaust Boot. Move 2 clearings." Law 9.5.1 (p.10): "Exhaust one M to move"; a move goes "to one adjacent clearing" (4.2, p.4). Fix: "Move 1 clearing."
7. **wrong rule** (Move tip). App: "Hostile means any enemy pieces present." Law 9.2.9.IIIb (p.9-10): "You must exhaust an extra M to move (even if forced) into a clearing with any warriors of any Hostile faction." Fix: "Extra boot only if the destination has warriors of a faction you are Hostile with."
8. **wrong number** (Explore tip). App: "Ruins have 3 items." Law 9.3.4 (p.10): "Place one beneath each ruin"; with two Vagabonds 9.7.1 (p.10-11): "place two random 'R' items in each ruin slot." Fix: "Each ruin holds 1 item (2 with two Vagabonds)."
9. **wrong rule** (Aid tip). App: "Can only aid when you have items to give! ... Hostile cannot be aided." Law 9.5.4 (p.10): "Exhaust any one item and give a card matching your clearing to a player with faction pieces there (even Hostile)"; 9.2.9.IIIc (p.10): "you can still Aid a Hostile faction to take crafted items." The item is exhausted, not given. Fix: "You give a card and exhaust an item; you can aid Hostile factions but their marker does not move."
10. **wrong number** (Repair / Craft, description and tip). App: "Craft: Exhaust 2 Hammers matching clearing suit." and "Crafting requires 2 Hammers!" Law 9.5.8 (p.10): "Craft a card by exhausting one H per crafting icon it has. Your clearing must match all of the card's crafting icons." Fix: "One hammer per crafting icon on the card."
11. **wrong reference** (battle_strike). App: `ruleReference: '9.5.2'` for Battle and Strike. Law: Strike is 9.5.6 (p.10). Fix: split or cite "9.5.2, 9.5.6".
12. **wrong reference** (repair_craft). App: `ruleReference: '9.5.7'` for Repair and Craft. Law: Craft is 9.5.8 (p.10). Fix: cite "9.5.7, 9.5.8".
13. **wrong rule** (Rest tip). App: "Damaged items are face-down." Law 9.2.7 (p.9): damaged items are moved "to his Damaged box"; 9.5.7 (p.10) repairs "keeping the item on its current side"; 9.2.5 (p.9): exhausting flips items face down. Face down means exhausted; damaged means in the Damaged box, either side. Fix the tip.
14. **wrong rule** (Evening draw). App: "Draw 1 card + 1 per Coin in satchel." Law 9.6.2 (p.10): "Draw one card, plus one card per X face up on the respective track." Coins are not kept in the Satchel while face up (9.2.5.I: "Whenever a T, X, or B is face up in your Satchel, place it in an empty matching track space"). Fix: "per face-up coin on the Coins track."
15. **wrong rule** (Satchel limit). App: "6 + 2 per Bag in satchel." Law 9.6.4 (p.10): "If you have more items total in your Satchel and Damaged box than your item limit, six plus two per B face up on the respective track, remove items ... from the game permanently." Fix: bags counted face up on the Bags track; the limit counts Satchel plus Damaged box.

Uncertain:
- Quest example "2 Swords for Attack quest": none of the 15 base quests in Cards (ROOT-31 to ROOT-45) is named Attack, and quest item costs are not in the sources. Also 9.5.5 (p.10) requires the quest suit to match your clearing, which the description omits.
