# Rules fix against the Law of Root (Oct 2025)

Follow-up to `audit-2026-09-27-law.md`. Branch `fix/law-oct-2025`, 2026-10-02.

## Sources

- **Law**: `Root_Base_Law_Oct_2025.pdf` (October 13, 2025). `src/data/law_full.ts` is generated from it by `scripts/extract_law.py` (701 rules, chapters 1 to 18 and appendices A, C, G, H, K, L, M, V).
- **Cards**: the Leder Games card library (cards.ledergames.com). Suits, crafting costs, items, points and texts of both decks, the 32 hireling cards and the 14 landmark cards were read from the card images.
- **Maps**: Winter from the base Learn to Play (p. 23), Lake and Mountain from the Underworld Learn to Play (p. 6, 7).

## What changed

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Faction guides | Setup and turn steps of 9 factions rewritten per Law chapters 6 and 8 to 15 (Eyrie had no errors) |
| `law_snippets.ts`, `rules_db.ts`, `glossary.ts`, `general_rules.ts` | Wrong mechanics and references corrected, numbering moved to the Oct 2025 edition |
| `law_full.ts` | Regenerated from the PDF; sub-rules have their own ids (`8.4.1.III`, `9.2.9.IIIb`) |
| Law search | A rule number matches that rule and its sub-rules only (`6.5.1` no longer finds `16.5.1`) |
| `cards.ts` | Regenerated: one entry per card design, 60 entries, real item card names |
| `hirelings.ts` | The 16 official hireling pairs with card text; ability timing per Law H.2 |
| Hireling tool | Control markers come from the control die on both sides; a hireling at 0 markers passes on |
| `landmarks.ts` | Card texts; the Legendary Forge tracker starts with the four items of the clearing's suit |
| `maps.ts` | Winter, Lake and Mountain modelled from the booklet pictures; Winter always deals random suits |
| AdSet draft | One shared pool (militant first, then one card per player), locked last insurgent, hireling factions excluded (Law A.8, A.6.5) |
| `setup_guide.ts` | Advanced Setup follows A.1 to A.10 |
| Battle wizard | Armorers ignores rolled hits only; the Vagabond's sword cap applies as defender too |
| Hundreds Hoard | Command and Prowess per 14.2.4.II, four items per track |
| Vagabond inventory | Character starting items (Appendix V), tracks, Damaged box, refresh, item limit, draw |

## Tests

`npm test`: 63 tests. New: every `ruleReference` in guides, glossary and `RULES_DB` resolves in `LAW_FULL`; map, deck, hireling and landmark invariants; AdSet draft rules; Law search; Vagabond item rules.

## Open items

- **Autumn map layout** in `maps.ts` is unchanged and unverified: no full picture of the Autumn board is in the sources.
- **Ruins on Lake and Mountain**: one of four is legible on the Lake picture (clearing 5), two on the Mountain picture (5 and 8). The rest need the physical board.
- **Building slot counts** on Winter, Lake and Mountain are read from small pictures and are not used by the app.
- **Copies per card** (`totalInDeck`) are set only for ambush and dominance cards (Law 2.1.2, 2.1.3); the card library lists designs, not copies.
- **Draw bonus tracks**: guides say "per uncovered draw bonus" as the Law does; the Law does not name the track.
- **AdSet draft** is still limited to 2 to 4 players in the UI; the Law allows any player count.
- **Homeland factions** (chapters 16 to 18) are in the Law viewer but have no guides.
